# Codebook reference (1.0-rc1)

Use these ids in the sheets; separate several values with semicolons.

## Regulatory orientation (`regulatoryOrientation`, one value)

- `precautionary`: Precautionary
- `balanced`: Balanced
- `innovation-first`: Innovation-first
- `unclear`: Unclear

## Level of government (`levelOfGovernment`, several allowed)

- `federal`: Federal
- `state`: State
- `international`: International
- `preemption`: Preemption stance

## Policy instruments (`instruments`, several allowed)

- `disclosure`: Disclosure
- `audits`: Audits
- `liability`: Liability
- `licensing`: Licensing
- `bans`: Bans
- `funding`: Funding
- `procurement`: Procurement rules
- `voluntary-standards`: Voluntary standards

## Issue areas (`issueAreas`, several allowed)

- `safety`: Safety
- `labor`: Labor
- `privacy`: Privacy
- `child-safety`: Child safety
- `deepfakes-elections`: Deepfakes and elections
- `discrimination`: Discrimination
- `national-security-china`: National security and China
- `energy-data-centers`: Energy and data centers
- `innovation-competitiveness`: Innovation and competitiveness

## Specificity (`specificity`, one value)

- `general-rhetoric`: General rhetoric
- `stated-principle`: Stated principle
- `concrete-proposal`: Concrete proposal
- `legislative-action`: Legislative action

## Evidence type (`evidenceType`, one value)

- `direct-quote`: Direct quote
- `reported`: Reported
- `voting-record`: Voting record
- `bill`: Bill
- `questionnaire`: Questionnaire response

## Policy categories (`categories`, several allowed)

- `dc-moratorium`: Moratoriums and bans
- `dc-ratepayer`: Electricity costs and grid
- `dc-tax-incentives`: Tax breaks
- `dc-local-control`: Siting and local control
- `dc-water-environment`: Water and environment
- `dc-economy-jobs`: Investment and jobs
- `dc-permitting`: Permitting and faster buildout
- `dc-security`: Security and foreign technology
- `dc-transparency`: Disclosure and studies
- `ai-comprehensive`: Broad AI governance laws
- `ai-frontier-safety`: Frontier and high-risk AI safety
- `ai-child-safety`: Children, chatbots and online safety
- `ai-deepfakes`: Deepfakes
- `ai-privacy-surveillance`: Privacy and surveillance
- `ai-copyright`: Copyright and creators
- `ai-sovereignty`: Digital sovereignty and European champions
- `ai-chips-china`: Chips and China
- `ai-national-security`: National security and defense
- `ai-preemption`: Federal vs. state authority
- `ai-consumer-protection`: Consumer protection and enforcement
- `ai-workers`: Workers and the AI economy
- `ai-innovation`: Innovation and public investment
